# Product Requirements (Snapshot)

The Live Vision is a mobile-first sports streaming web app with three core areas:
- Stream: play embedded links and search live events.
- Events: browse fixtures with filters and quick stream buttons.
- Channels: browse live channels with country filters.

## UX Goals
- App-like responsiveness on mobile.
- Clear navigation between pages.
- Consistent card-based layouts with minimal clutter.

## Design Notes
- Keep the logo on the left and the theme toggle on the right on mobile.
- Ensure 44px tap targets.
- Use official team/league logos when available.

## Data Sources
- beta.adstrim.ru for streams, events, and channels.
